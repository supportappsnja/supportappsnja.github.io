Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

This policy describes how the Orbit Snake app (“the App”) handles information. It is written to match the App’s current behavior.

Who we are

Orbit Snake is published under the package / bundle id com.appsnja.orbitsnake. Questions: support.appsnja@gmail.com.

User data

We collect no user data.

Information stored by the App

The App keeps a small amount of local app data (for example via the browser / WebView localStorage), including:

  • Best score (classic mode)
  • Turbo mode best score
  • Daily Challenge best score, today’s date key, attempt count, and a capped local history of past daily scores
  • Games-played counter and coach / first-run tip progress
  • Sound mute preference
  • Vibration / haptics preference
  • A local cache of whether Orbit Premium is active
  • Local premium soft-prompt timing state

We collect no user data from this local app data.

Purchases and payments

Optional Orbit Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold only through Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple StoreKit (iOS). Product id: orbit_premium_monthly. Payment card details and store account information are handled entirely by Google or Apple. We never see or store your payment card details.

On web / development builds, billing may be mocked so the game can be tested without a real charge.

Ads and analytics

The App has no ads in v1: no advertising SDK, no ad network requests, and no ad identifiers collected.

The App does not include a third-party analytics SDK. If that changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that change ships.

Children

Orbit Snake is a casual arcade game with no social features. We collect no user data and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents / guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example via the contact email) may contact support.appsnja@gmail.com and we will delete that correspondence from our inbox where reasonably possible.

Store age ratings and parental controls are determined by Google Play and the App Store; please use those platform tools as appropriate.

Permissions and platform services

On mobile, the App may use platform features such as haptics / vibration, status bar / system bars, splash screen, the system share sheet, and store billing APIs provided by Capacitor and the OS. Score share writes a generated score-card image to on-device cache and hands it to the system share sheet. We collect no user data from these features.

Links the App may open include the Privacy Policy and Terms pages hosted at https://supportappsnja.github.io/orbit-snake/, and the platform subscription-management pages when you choose Manage subscription.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes (for example if a real ad or analytics SDK is added). The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Privacy questions: support.appsnja@gmail.com.
